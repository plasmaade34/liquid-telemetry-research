open-sourcing it was a deliberate choice and it stays open. Going forward,
AGPLv3 keeps new work genuinely open for research, students, and
non-commercial use, while giving companies that want to use it in a
closed commercial product a real reason to negotiate a license instead of
just taking it for free. This is not intended to make anything less
available to the open-source community -- AGPLv3 is a real open-source
license, not a restriction on non-commercial use.
